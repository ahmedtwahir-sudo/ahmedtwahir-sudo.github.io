ABOUT.

ShikaDeal was founded in Kilifi, Kenya by Ahmed Twahir.

Good deals are all around us, but many never get the attention they deserve. Some quietly benefit the few people who know where to find them, while others are gone by the time you hear about them.

That’s where ShikaDeal comes in.

Our goal is simple: to share deals, useful finds, and opportunities that the average Kenyan might not hear about — and make it easier to get them before they’re gone.

Everything you’ll find here comes from the same simple idea: find good stuff, make cool stuff, and share it with you.

Some products are sourced and ready to ship. Others are digital guides we’ve created, or 3D-printed items made on demand.

It’s a mix of interesting deals, practical finds, digital products, and things we genuinely think are worth checking out.

Discover something good. Get it while you can. Enjoy the benefit.
